Common questions

How do I delete my account and data?

Open Monetra, go to the Settings tab, and tap Delete Account under the Account section. This permanently removes your account and every product, order, sale, and expense you've recorded — this can't be undone.

I forgot my password. How do I get back in?

On the Settings tab, tap Forgot Password and we'll send a reset link to the email you signed up with. If you don't see it within a few minutes, check your spam folder.

What's included in the free plan?

Free accounts can record up to 50 orders a month. Everything else — viewing orders and receipts, Insights charts, customer history, and CSV import — is free with no limit. Premium removes the monthly order cap.

How do I change my business's currency?

Tap the logo in the top-right corner of any screen to open your Business Profile, then choose from the currency chips or type any 3-letter ISO code (like AUD or SGD). Every amount in the app updates immediately.

Can I import records from a spreadsheet?

Yes. From Settings, choose Import Data and pick a CSV file of products, sales, or expenses. We'll show you exactly what will be imported before anything is saved.

Is my business data private?

Yes — your products, orders, and expenses are stored under your account only and no other Monetra user can read or write them. See our Privacy Policy for details.

I found a bug or have a feature request.

We'd love to hear it — email us at the address above with what happened (or what you'd like to see) and, if it's a bug, what device and iOS version you're on.

Monetra is built and supported independently. We typically reply within 1–2 business days.
